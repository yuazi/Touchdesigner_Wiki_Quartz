## (Who Am I)

Hey, I'm **Yus**.

I study *Wirtschaftsinformatik* — what happens when a computer science degree and a business degree have a child and nobody wins.

This garden is my way of thinking out loud. I learn best when I can *see* things — diagrams, connections, visual structures — so this space grew out of that habit. Writing things down here helps me understand them. If it helps you too, that's a bonus.

You'll find notes on creative tech, visual programming, tools I use, and whatever else has been occupying my brain lately. Nothing here is finished. That's intentional.
