TOPs are TouchDesigner's **2D image and video processing** operators, running entirely on the GPU. They handle everything visual — loading video, compositing layers, applying effects, and serving as the final output stage for 3D renders. Pay close attention to resolution and pixel format settings, as they significantly affect quality and performance.

**Key nodes:** Movie File In, Constant, Composite, Blur, Level, Render
