This module covers advanced hardware I/O, network protocols, and custom GPU programming.

## Connectivity & Protocols
- **[[OSC and MIDI]]**: Sending and receiving control data.
- **[[NDI and Syphon]]**: Streaming real-time video across networks or between applications on the same machine.
- **[[DMX and Art-Net]]**: Controlling physical lighting fixtures and LEDs.
- **[[Audio Reactivity]]**: Analyzing sound streams to drive generative visuals.

## Custom Shaders
- **[[Introduction to GLSL]]**: Writing custom vertex and pixel shaders in TouchDesigner to bypass node overhead and execute native GPU code.
