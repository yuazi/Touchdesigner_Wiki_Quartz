> [!abstract] Course Summary
> VR and AR sits across graphics, tracking, computer vision, interaction, hardware, and human perception. The course pulls those threads together into systems that are responsive, comfortable, and registered to the real world.

## Lecture Notes

- [[/notes/lectures/virtualaugmentedreality/00_VR-AR|(y-00) Course Organization]]  
   Course philosophy, structure, project, quizzes, and oral exam requirements.

- [[/notes/lectures/virtualaugmentedreality/01_VR-AR|(y-01) Introduction and History]]  
   VR/AR definitions, immersion and presence, history, applications, and the mixed reality continuum.

- [[/notes/lectures/virtualaugmentedreality/02_VR-AR_hcd|(y-02) Human-Centered Design]]  
   Iterative design, analysis, constraints, VR-specific requirements, prototyping, and testing.

- [[/notes/lectures/virtualaugmentedreality/03-1_VR-AR_hardware|(y-03.1) Stereo Rendering and Hardware]]  
   Depth cues, stereo rendering, parallax, off-axis projection, HMD optics, latency, and display challenges.

- [[/notes/lectures/virtualaugmentedreality/03-2_VR-AR_hardware-2|(y-03.2) AR Displays and Input Hardware]]  
   Optical/video see-through AR, display-space taxonomy, spatial AR, haptics, and multimodal input.

- [[/notes/lectures/virtualaugmentedreality/04_VR-AR_Interaction|(y-04) Interaction in VR and AR]]  
   3D interaction tasks, selection, manipulation, navigation, system control, and AR-specific interaction.

- [[/notes/lectures/virtualaugmentedreality/05_VR-AR_tracking|(y-05) Tracking in VR and AR]]  
   Registration, calibration, tracking, coordinate systems, sensor technologies, optical tracking, and sensor fusion.

- [[/notes/lectures/virtualaugmentedreality/06_VR-AR_CompVision|(y-06) Computer Vision for AR]]  
   Marker tracking, homographies, multi-camera IR tracking, natural features, SIFT, pose estimation, SLAM, and outdoor tracking.

- [[/notes/lectures/virtualaugmentedreality/07_VR-AR_Calibration|(y-07) Calibration and Registration]]  
   Camera calibration (lens distortion, undistortion, targets), display calibration (shooting gallery, boresight, SPAAM, pointing device, eye tracking), hand-eye calibration, and registration errors from error propagation and latency.
