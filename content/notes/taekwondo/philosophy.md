Taekwondo is more than just kicking; it's a way of life (Do). The "Do" in Taekwondo means "the way" or "the path."

## The Five Tenets of Taekwondo

These are the core values every student is expected to live by:

1.  **Courtesy (Ye Ui):** Be polite to everyone, regardless of rank.
2.  **Integrity (Yom Chi):** Know right from wrong. Be honest with yourself and others.
3.  **Perseverance (In Nae):** Never give up, no matter how hard the training or life gets.
4.  **Self-Control (Guk Gi):** Control your emotions and your physical power.
5.  **Indomitable Spirit (Baekjul Boolgool):** Have the courage to stand up for what is right, even against overwhelming odds.

## The Student Oath

- I shall observe the tenets of Taekwondo.
- I shall respect the instructor and seniors.
- I shall never misuse Taekwondo.
- I shall be a champion of freedom and justice.
- I shall build a more peaceful world.
