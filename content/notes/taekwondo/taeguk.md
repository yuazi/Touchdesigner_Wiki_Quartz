The Taegeuk poomsae are the foundational patterns of World Taekwondo (WT). Each of the eight Taegeuk forms represents a specific philosophical symbol (_palgwae_) and introduces progressively more complex techniques and stances.

---

## Taegeuk 1: Il Jang (Heaven and Light)

Represents the beginning of the universe and the "birth" of a martial artist.
