## Mental Model First: From Theory to Interaction

Virtual Reality is not just about "better graphics." It's about **Human-Centered Design**. This course is designed to move you from understanding the hardware (Headsets, Tracking) to actually building an interactive experience. The focus is **Project-Based Learning**.

---

## Course Structure: Blended Learning

The course follows a "Blended Learning" model:
- **Tuesdays (Lecture)**: "Classical" theoretical lectures on the physics and psychology of VR/AR.
- **Thursdays (Exercise)**: Mandatory, interactive sessions. This includes Unity tutorials, quizzes, and project presentations.
- **Participation is Key**: You gather points throughout the semester through quizzes and project status reports.

---

## The Team Project (50% of Grade)

The core of the course is a team project (usually 3 students). You are given full creative freedom to define your own topic, or you can choose from inspiration provided by the lecturers.

### Key Milestones:
1. **Status Reports**: Regular oral updates in class to the lecturers and peers.
2. **Final Submission**: 
   - **Software**: The code for your interactive tool (Unity is the industry standard here).
   - **Video**: A 2-minute "pitch" video showing your project in action.
   - **Report**: A 4-5 page written report following the **VIS-TVCG** academic template.
3. **Live Demo**: A final presentation held at **VISUS**, where others can try your experience.

### Hardware Access:
Students can borrow professional-grade hardware from the VISUS department, including:
- **HMDs**: Meta Quest, HTC Vive, Valve Index.
- **Tracking Systems**: SteamVR Base Stations, OptiTrack.
- **Cleaning Systems**: Important for hygiene in VR labs.

---

## Grading Breakdown (The 100-Point System)

You accumulate points through two parallel tracks:

| Category | Points | Description |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Project** | 50 pts | Status reports, final demo, code, video, and written report. |
| **Quizzes** | 50 pts | Six bi-weekly quizzes (10 min each, multi-choice). Best 5 count. |

### The Final Exam:
- **Prerequisite**: You must gather at least **60 points** (Project + Quizzes) to be eligible for the oral exam.
- **The Oral Exam**: A 20-minute team interview. Questions focus on the theoretical parts of the course and your project implementation.

---

## Recommended Tools & Skills

- **Unity Engine**: The primary platform for project development. C# knowledge is a major advantage.
- **Visual Computing Foundations**: Having a background in Computer Graphics, HCI, and Computer Vision will be extremely helpful for the technical aspects of tracking and registration.
